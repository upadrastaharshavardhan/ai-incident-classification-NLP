Research Problem

Turning incident text into operational intelligence.

IT organizations process large volumes of unstructured incident descriptions. Understanding category, urgency, and historical context manually creates delays and inconsistency.

🏷️

Incident Classification

Automatically identify the most likely operational category from natural-language incident descriptions.

🚨

Priority Prediction

Estimate incident urgency to support faster triage, queue optimization, and escalation decisions.

🔎

Similar Incident Retrieval

Find historically related incidents so engineers can reuse knowledge and investigate recurring problems faster.
